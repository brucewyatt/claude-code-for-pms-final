# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

Did anyone say something that nobody else mentioned?

### 2.

Grading off frequency and severity, what is the most urgent issue that was raised from the interviews?

### 3.

How does this interview feedback compare with the breakdown that we've produced in CLAUDE.md? Without changing either source, compare and contrast interview feedback with internal notes and context to create a general statement about the issue with release 4.2. 

Put it in general language: "Based on the interviews, the trouble with 4.2 is _".

### 4.

How do these tickets differ from the interview feedback? What is the overlap in terms of those who filed the tickets and those who responded to our interviews?

### 5.

What are the overlapping themes between interviews and tickets? Make a visualization (table or plot) to help easily communicate our best areas of focus.

### 6.

Who filed these? Give me a list of names and how many of them filed each.
Remove all duplicates and then give me a list of prioritized themes that are grouped as earlier. 

### 7.

Only look at what came in after 12 August. What's different about these compared to everything before?

### 8.

What's your hypothesis as to what the tickets are telling you the issue is here?

### 9.

To help me better understand and explain it to others, please explain it at multiple levels. Explain it to me like I'm a 5th grader, a high school sophomore, a freshman college student, and a professional.

### 10.

Should interviews or tickets get priority where they disagree or contradict each other?

### 11.

If I'd only read the interviews, what would I have got wrong?

### 12.

If I'd only read the tickets, what would I have got wrong?

### 13.

Now I want to fine tune my hypothesis from before. Please help me refine what exactly the problem is based on interviews and tickets.
